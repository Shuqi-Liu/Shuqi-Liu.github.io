Shuqi Liu, PhD. Research on aging, motor learning, and walking.

About me

I'm open to research scientist roles in academia and industry, and to postdoctoral positions in labs or companies that offer a path to a research scientist role.

I am a postdoctoral associate at the University of Pittsburgh. I study how aging affects motor learning in walking, combining EMG, motion capture, and brain imaging (fNIRS and MRI) with statistical modeling such as hierarchical regression, canonical correlation analysis, and PCA.

I also bring industry experience. Before graduate school, I spent two and a half years as a software engineer at Meso Scale Diagnostics, building Java software for scientific data analysis and visualization. I earned my PhD in Bioengineering at Pitt in 2026, and my undergraduate degree is in mathematics with a minor in computer science.
